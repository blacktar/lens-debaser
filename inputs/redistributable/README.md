# Redistribution-approved validation inputs

The project owner has explicitly confirmed that the files in this directory
may be included with and redistributed by Lens Debaser. They are deliberately
separated from `inputs/Reference_Lenses`, whose downloaded third-party lens
material is for local research and is not approved for redistribution here.

the input suited to the behaviour under review: real footage for perceptual
effects, the ISO chart for detail and aberration, and the OGC/TERA chart for
geometry and field behaviour.

## Project-generated imagery

The project owner has also approved wholly synthetic test charts, test images
and reference images generated by Codex specifically for Lens Debaser for
inclusion and redistribution. This approval applies to the generated image
content, but does not require every historical render to be committed; retain
only assets that have an ongoing validation, documentation or demonstration
purpose.

This approval does not automatically cover downloaded third-party material or
an output that contains, reproduces or is derived from third-party source
imagery. Those assets remain local unless their redistribution rights are
separately established or the project owner explicitly approves them.
