Implemented optical-model design references
-------------------------------------------

Lens Debaser's optical model is an original real-time approximation; no source
code from the publications below is incorporated. The production chromatic
pipeline uses their relevant image-formation concepts: a common optical point-
spread response, wavelength-dependent RGB responses, smooth spatially varying
filter support, and chromatic displacement applied to the optically softened
image rather than to sharp display-referred channels.

Jeong et al., "Chromatic Aberration Rendering for a Physically-based Camera"
  https://cg.skku.edu/pub/papers/2016-jeong-cgi-chroma-cam.pdf

Mantiuk et al., "Rendering Algorithms for Aberrated Human Vision Simulation"
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10023823/

Bauer et al., "Fast Two-step Blind Optical Aberration Correction"
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.00950

NVIDIA Research, "Image-Space Gathering"
  https://research.nvidia.com/sites/default/files/pubs/2009-08_Image-Space-Gathering/HPG09-ISG.pdf
